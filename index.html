How to Experience the AR Tour

1

Scan QR Code

Scan the code placed at the entrance or near exhibition points using your phone camera.

2

Allow Camera

Grant camera permissions in your browser to enable spatial tracking.

3

Point & Discover

Point your camera at the designated area. The animated character will pop up to share historical insights.

4

Close AR

Tap the close button on your screen whenever you are finished to exit the AR view.
